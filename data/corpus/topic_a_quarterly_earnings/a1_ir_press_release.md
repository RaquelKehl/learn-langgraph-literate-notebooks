# Solvane Dynamics reports results for the third quarter of 2025

Winterthur, 4 November 2025. Solvane Dynamics AG (fictional), the industrial automation company headquartered in Winterthur and listed under the ticker SVDY, today published its results for the third quarter of 2025. The company recorded strong demand across its core markets and continued to improve profitability.

## Key figures

- Revenue Q3 2025: CHF 342 million (Q3 2024: CHF 298 million), an increase of 14.8% year on year.
- EBITDA margin: 22.3% (Q3 2024: 19.7%).
- Net profit: CHF 41 million (Q3 2024: CHF 33 million).
- Net debt: CHF 210 million, equivalent to 1.4 times EBITDA.

## Comment from management

Elena Marti, Chief Financial Officer of Solvane Dynamics, said: "Our growth this quarter came mainly from the expansion of our industrial automation business, where customers continue to invest in modern production lines. The improvement in our margin reflects economies of scale in production, as higher volumes allowed us to spread fixed costs across a larger output. We are pleased that this progress is visible in both revenue and profit."

She added that the company would remain disciplined in its investment decisions and would continue to focus on operational efficiency across all production sites.

## Outlook

Based on the results of the first nine months, Solvane Dynamics confirms its guidance for full-year 2025 revenue growth of 12% to 15%. The company will report its full-year results in line with its published financial calendar.

For further information, please contact the Investor Relations department of Solvane Dynamics AG in Winterthur.
