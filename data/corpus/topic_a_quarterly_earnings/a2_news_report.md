# Solvane Dynamics beats expectations as automation demand lifts revenue

Solvane Dynamics AG (fictional) delivered a stronger third quarter than most market watchers had expected, as factories kept spending on automation despite a patchy industrial backdrop.

The Winterthur-based company, listed under the ticker SVDY, reported revenue of CHF 342 million for the third quarter of 2025, up 14.8% year on year from CHF 298 million. Net profit came in at CHF 41 million. The results were published on 4 November 2025.

Speaking on the earnings call, Chief Financial Officer Elena Marti pointed to customer demand as the main driver. "Manufacturers are no longer treating automation as a project for later; they are ordering now, and our order book shows it," she told analysts. She also said the company had seen broad interest across its customer base rather than reliance on a few large contracts.

Investors welcomed the numbers. Solvane Dynamics shares rose 3.1% to CHF 87.40 on 4 November 2025, making the stock one of the stronger performers among Swiss industrial names that day.

Analysts focused less on the top line than on profitability. Jonas Feld, analyst at Talgrund Securities (fictional), called the margin improvement "the real story of the quarter", arguing that it showed the company could grow without sacrificing discipline on costs.

Management confirmed its guidance for full-year 2025 revenue growth of 12% to 15%. The company did not provide a figure for the fourth quarter, and executives declined to comment on individual customer orders during the call.
