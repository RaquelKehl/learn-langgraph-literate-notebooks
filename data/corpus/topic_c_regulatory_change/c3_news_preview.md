# Regulator expected to tighten crypto custody rules

The Alpine Markets Supervisory Authority (fictional), known as AMSA, is expected to publish new custody rules for crypto assets in the coming weeks, according to people familiar with the matter.

The rules would apply to banks and securities firms that hold crypto assets on behalf of their clients. People familiar with the matter say the focus is on protecting client assets, in particular in the event that a firm runs into difficulties. The regulator is said to have held informal talks with market participants over the summer.

Details and timing are not yet known. The people familiar with the matter declined to say which specific measures are under discussion or when any new rules would take effect. AMSA did not respond to a request for comment.

Industry representatives said firms in the sector were watching closely. Several smaller providers are understood to be concerned about the cost of adapting their systems, while larger banks are said to be better prepared. Lawyers who advise the sector said that firms would only be able to plan properly once the final text is published.

The publication would mark the first time that AMSA sets out dedicated rules on the custody of crypto assets for client holdings.
