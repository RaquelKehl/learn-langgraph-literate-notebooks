# AMSA Circular 2025/04: Custody of crypto assets held for clients

Issued by the Alpine Markets Supervisory Authority (fictional), hereinafter AMSA, on 15 September 2025.

## Scope

Margin no. 1: This circular applies to banks and securities firms that hold crypto assets in custody on behalf of clients.

Margin no. 2: For the purposes of this circular, client crypto assets are all crypto assets that a firm holds in custody for a client and over which the client holds the economic entitlement.

Margin no. 3: The requirements of this circular apply irrespective of whether the firm holds the crypto assets itself or through a sub-custodian. Where a sub-custodian is used, the firm remains responsible for compliance with this circular.

## Requirements

Margin no. 4: Firms within the scope of this circular shall meet the following five requirements:

1. Client crypto assets are held segregated from the firm's own assets.
2. At least 90% of client crypto assets are kept in offline cold storage.
3. Client holdings are reconciled at least once every business day.
4. Custody arrangements are audited by an independent auditor once a year.
5. Clients are informed in writing which custody model applies to them.

Margin no. 5: Firms shall document how they meet each of these requirements and shall make this documentation available to AMSA on request.

Margin no. 6: The segregation required under Margin no. 4 shall ensure that client crypto assets can be identified at all times and are protected in the event of the firm's insolvency.

Margin no. 7: The written information to clients shall describe the custody model in clear and understandable terms and shall be provided before the custody relationship begins.

## Entry into force

Margin no. 8: This circular applies from 1 July 2026.

Margin no. 9: For client relationships existing before 1 July 2026, a transition period runs until 31 December 2026. By the end of the transition period, firms shall meet all requirements of this circular for these relationships as well.

Margin no. 10: AMSA will assess compliance with this circular as part of its ongoing supervision.
