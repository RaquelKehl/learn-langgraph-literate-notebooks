# Crypto custody: why Swiss firms should prepare for stricter rules

At Keller Amsler Legal (fictional), we advise banks and securities firms on every aspect of digital assets. One question comes up in almost every client conversation at the moment: what will the regulator expect from firms that hold crypto assets for their clients?

The Alpine Markets Supervisory Authority (fictional), known as AMSA, has signalled that custody rules for crypto assets will be tightened. While the details have not been set out, the direction of travel is clear. Firms that start preparing now will be in a far better position than those that wait for the final text.

## What firms can do today

Review segregation. Make sure you can show, at any moment, which crypto assets belong to clients and which belong to the firm. If your records rely on manual workarounds, now is the time to fix them.

Strengthen governance. Custody of crypto assets should have clear ownership at management level, with defined responsibilities, regular reporting and an escalation path when something goes wrong.

Update client documentation. Clients should understand how their crypto assets are held and what protection they enjoy. Clear, plain language documentation builds trust and will help you respond quickly when stricter rules arrive.
