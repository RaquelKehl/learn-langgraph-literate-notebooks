# This week in wealth tech

Welcome to this week's issue. Our lead item comes from the FinTech Pulse (fictional) blog, followed by two quick updates from around the industry.

## Robo-advice keeps getting cheaper

The average robo-advisory fee in Switzerland has fallen to just 0.38% a year, according to the latest industry figures. (via FinTech Pulse)

Good news for cost-conscious investors, and a clear sign that competition among digital platforms is working.

## A cleaner investing app

Alpenvest (fictional) has released a redesigned version of its investing app. The update brings a cleaner portfolio overview, simpler onboarding for new clients and clearer notifications when a portfolio is rebalanced. Early reviews from users praise the faster navigation and the new dark mode.

## A new head of product

Kornhaus Digital (fictional) has appointed a new head of product to lead the development of its digital investment offering. The company says the appointment will help it bring new features to clients more quickly and support its plans to grow its engineering team in Zurich.

That is all for this week. See you next Tuesday.
