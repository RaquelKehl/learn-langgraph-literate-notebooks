# Robo-advice in Switzerland is now almost free

Remember when investing your savings meant a meeting at the bank, a thick folder of brochures and a bill that ate into your returns year after year? Those days are disappearing fast.

The average robo-advisory fee in Switzerland has fallen to just 0.38% a year, according to the latest industry figures.

That is a remarkable result for a market that many traditional players dismissed as a niche not so long ago. Robo-advisers have done exactly what digital challengers are supposed to do: they have made investing simpler, more transparent and much cheaper.

## Competition is doing its job

The reason is simple: competition. New platforms keep entering the market, and every new entrant has to offer something better than the last. Established providers have had to respond by cutting prices, simplifying their offers and improving their apps. The winners are ordinary investors, who can now build a diversified portfolio from their phone in a few minutes.

## What this means for you

If you have been holding back because you thought professional portfolio management was only for the wealthy, it is time to look again. Robo-advice has become an everyday product, and the cost argument against it has all but vanished.

We expect the price war to continue. Swiss investors have never had it so good, and we will keep tracking which platforms lead the way.
