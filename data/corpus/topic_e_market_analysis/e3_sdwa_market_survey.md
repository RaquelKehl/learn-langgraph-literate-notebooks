# Robo-Advisory Market Survey 2025

The Swiss Digital Wealth Association (fictional), hereinafter SDWA, publishes this annual survey to provide investors, providers and policymakers with a reliable picture of the Swiss robo-advisory market. This edition covers pricing and market size for 2025.

## Key findings

- Number of robo-advisory platforms surveyed: 14.
- Average all-in annual fee: 0.62% of assets (2024: 0.68%), asset-weighted.
- Lowest fee observed: 0.38%.
- Highest fee observed: 0.95%.
- Assets managed by robo-advisers: CHF 9.4 billion (2024: CHF 7.9 billion).

## Commentary

Pricing in the Swiss robo-advisory market continued to move downwards in 2025. The decline in the average all-in annual fee reflects both competition between established providers and the entry of new platforms with simplified offerings. At the same time, the gap between the lowest and the highest fee observed remains wide, which shows that providers continue to differentiate themselves through service levels, investment approach and the degree of personal advice they offer.

Investors should note that the lowest fee observed applies to a single platform and does not represent the market as a whole. The average all-in annual fee, weighted by assets, is the most meaningful measure of what Swiss investors actually pay for robo-advisory services.

Assets managed by robo-advisers grew further over the year, supported by new client inflows and positive market performance. SDWA expects the market to keep developing as digital investment becomes more familiar to a broader range of investors.

## Methodology

SDWA conducted a survey of robo-advisory platforms active in Switzerland in July and August 2025. Each platform reported its all-in annual fee, covering portfolio management, safekeeping of assets and transaction costs, together with its assets under management. Fees were weighted by assets under management to calculate the average, so that larger platforms carry a correspondingly larger weight. Where platforms offer several pricing tiers, the tier applicable to the typical client portfolio was used.

SDWA thanks all participating platforms for their cooperation.
