# Investor Cost Monitor 2025: what automated investing really costs

The Alpine Consumer Finance Foundation (fictional) publishes its Investor Cost Monitor once a year. The foundation is financed by its members and accepts no money from the providers it tests. This edition looks at automated digital investment services, which build and rebalance a portfolio for clients on the basis of a short online questionnaire.

## How we tested

Between September and October 2025, our testers opened real accounts with 12 automated digital investment services that serve private clients in Switzerland. For each service, we invested a model portfolio of CHF 50,000 with a balanced risk profile and recorded every charge over the full period: management charges, safekeeping costs, product costs of the underlying funds and currency conversion. We then expressed the total as a percentage of invested assets per year and weighted each service by its share of the invested assets in our sample.

## Key result

- Mean all-in charge of automated digital investment services: 0.62% of invested assets per year

## What this means for investors

Automated investing has become cheaper, but it is not close to free. The headline prices that some providers advertise often cover only the management charge and leave out fund costs, which can make up a large part of the total. The cheapest service in a market says little about what a typical client pays.

Our advice: before opening an account, ask for the total cost in francs for your own portfolio, not just a percentage for one component. Compare at least three providers, and check whether currency conversion is included.
