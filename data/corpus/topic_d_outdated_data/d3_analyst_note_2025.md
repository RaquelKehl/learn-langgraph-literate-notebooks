# Brunnhof: lower payout buys flexibility, rating Hold

Lindmatt Research (fictional). Analyst: Reto Imhof.

Brunnhof Versicherungen AG (fictional) has cut its payout ratio target from 60% to 45% of net profit, effective from financial year 2025. The change was announced with the half-year results and replaces the target the insurer set at its Capital Markets Day in 2023.

## Our view

The decision will disappoint dividend investors. A lower payout target means lower income for shareholders who hold the stock mainly for its distributions, and we would not be surprised to see some of them rotate into other Swiss insurers.

On the other hand, the move buys Brunnhof flexibility. Management wants to fund growth in health insurance and strengthen its capital buffers, and retaining more earnings should leave the company with a stronger balance sheet. We regard this as a sensible long-term choice, even if it is unwelcome in the short term.

Share buybacks remain possible on a case-by-case basis, but we do not expect them to make up for the reduced dividend in the near term.

## Rating

We rate Brunnhof a Hold. The stock offers less income than before, but a stronger balance sheet and a clearer growth path in health insurance. We would revisit our rating once the company shows tangible progress in its health insurance business.
