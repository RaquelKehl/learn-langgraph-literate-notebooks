# Brunnhof promises investors a 60% payout

Brunnhof Versicherungen AG (fictional) wants to hand a larger share of its earnings back to shareholders. At its Capital Markets Day in Bern on 12 September 2023, the insurer set a payout ratio target of 60% of net profit under its new strategy Brunnhof 2026.

The company also committed to a progressive dividend, aiming to keep the dividend per share stable or rising every year over the strategy period, which runs from 2023 to 2026.

Chief Financial Officer Thomas Gerber presented the target with confidence. "Our capital strength allows us to offer shareholders an attractive and reliable return," he told investors. He added that the business was in good shape to deliver the earnings needed to support the policy.

The announcement is good news for income investors, who have been looking for dependable returns in the Swiss insurance sector. Brunnhof's clear commitment gives shareholders visibility on what they can expect over the coming years, and the company's solid capital base suggests the target is well within reach.
