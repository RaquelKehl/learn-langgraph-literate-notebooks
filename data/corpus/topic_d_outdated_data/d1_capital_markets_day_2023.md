# Brunnhof Capital Markets Day 2023: strategy Brunnhof 2026

Bern, 12 September 2023. At today's Capital Markets Day, Brunnhof Versicherungen AG (fictional), the insurer headquartered in Bern, presented its strategy Brunnhof 2026 to investors and analysts. The strategy covers the period 2023 to 2026 and sets out how the company intends to grow profitably while rewarding its shareholders.

## Strategic priorities

Brunnhof will concentrate on its core insurance business in Switzerland, deepen relationships with its existing customers and simplify its processes through targeted investment in digital tools. Management emphasised that the strategy builds on the strong position the company has achieved in recent years and on a solid capital base.

The executive board outlined three areas of focus: disciplined underwriting, a better experience for customers and agents, and efficient operations across all business lines.

## Capital and dividend policy

As part of strategy Brunnhof 2026, the company has set a payout ratio target of 60% of net profit. Brunnhof is committed to a progressive dividend, meaning that it aims to maintain or increase the dividend per share each year.

Thomas Gerber, Chief Financial Officer of Brunnhof Versicherungen, said: "Our capital strength allows us to offer shareholders an attractive and reliable return. With a payout ratio target of 60% of net profit and a progressive dividend, we are giving investors a clear commitment for the entire strategy period. We are confident that our business will generate the earnings to support it."

## Next steps

Brunnhof will report on progress against the strategy with its regular annual and half-year results. The presentation materials from the Capital Markets Day are available from the Investor Relations department.
