# Brunnhof adjusts capital policy with half-year results 2025

Bern, 26 August 2025. Brunnhof Versicherungen AG (fictional), the insurer headquartered in Bern, today published its half-year results 2025 and announced an adjustment to its capital policy.

## New payout ratio target

The Board of Directors has set a new payout ratio target of 45% of net profit from financial year 2025. The new target replaces the 60% target set in 2023 as part of the strategy Brunnhof 2026.

## Reasons for the adjustment

The adjustment allows Brunnhof to fund growth in health insurance, where the company sees attractive opportunities to expand its position in the coming years. At the same time, retaining a larger share of earnings will strengthen the company's capital buffers and increase its resilience to market volatility and changing regulatory demands.

Share buybacks remain possible on a case-by-case basis, depending on capital position, market conditions and investment opportunities at the time.

## Comment from management

Thomas Gerber, Chief Financial Officer of Brunnhof Versicherungen, said: "Setting our payout ratio target at 45% of net profit is a deliberate choice. It gives us the means to invest in health insurance, where we see real potential, and it strengthens our capital buffers. Shareholders will continue to participate in our success, and we remain committed to a reliable and attractive return over time."
