# Talvero Bank and Nordquist Privatbank agree to combine

Zurich and Lucerne, 20 October 2025. Talvero Bank AG (fictional), headquartered in Zurich, and Nordquist Privatbank AG (fictional), headquartered in Lucerne, today announced an all-share merger on 20 October 2025.

Under the agreed terms, Nordquist shareholders will receive 0.85 Talvero shares for each Nordquist share. The transaction has an implied transaction value of CHF 1.2 billion. The combined bank will have combined client assets of CHF 48 billion.

The two banks target annual cost synergies of CHF 65 million by the end of 2028. They expect one-off integration costs of CHF 90 million. Completion is expected in the first half of 2026, subject to regulatory and shareholder approval.

The combined bank will be called Talvero Nordquist AG (fictional) and will be led by Daniel Aebischer, the current Chief Executive Officer of Talvero Bank.

## Strategic rationale

The merger creates a stronger player in Swiss private banking. Greater scale will allow the combined bank to invest more in advisory quality, technology and client service. The two banks have complementary regional presence: Talvero is strongly rooted in the Zurich area, while Nordquist has a long tradition in Lucerne and central Switzerland. Both banks already work with a shared investment platform, which will make it easier to bring client offerings together.

Daniel Aebischer, Chief Executive Officer of Talvero Bank, said: "This is a natural fit. Together we will offer our clients a broader regional network and a single, proven investment platform, while keeping the personal service they value."

Sabine Wehrli, Chief Executive Officer of Nordquist Privatbank, said: "Our clients and employees gain a strong partner with the scale to keep investing in private banking. We are proud to bring our heritage into the new bank."

## Next steps

The merger requires the approval of the relevant regulatory authorities and of the shareholders of both banks at extraordinary general meetings. Both boards of directors unanimously support the transaction. Until completion, Talvero Bank and Nordquist Privatbank will continue to operate as independent banks.
