# Talvero Bank: merger with Nordquist strengthens the franchise, rating Buy

Rheinfeld Equity Research (fictional). Analyst: Nora Baumli.

We rate Talvero Bank AG (fictional) a Buy with a price target of CHF 64. The planned combination with Nordquist Privatbank AG (fictional) strengthens the franchise and, in our view, the market still underestimates the earnings potential of the deal.

## The terms

Talvero Bank AG, headquartered in Zurich, and Nordquist Privatbank AG, headquartered in Lucerne, announced an all-share merger on 20 October 2025. Nordquist shareholders receive 0.85 Talvero shares for each Nordquist share, giving an implied transaction value of CHF 1.2 billion. The combined bank will have combined client assets of CHF 48 billion. Management targets annual cost synergies of CHF 65 million by the end of 2028, against one-off integration costs of CHF 90 million. Completion is expected in the first half of 2026, subject to regulatory and shareholder approval. The combined bank will be called Talvero Nordquist AG (fictional) and will be led by Talvero's CEO Daniel Aebischer.

## Why the synergy target is credible

We regard the CHF 65 million target as credible. The two banks have a clear branch overlap in central Switzerland, where several locations serve the same towns and can be consolidated without leaving client regions uncovered. Both banks also already run a common investment platform, so the most complex part of a private banking integration, aligning portfolio management and product shelves, is largely in place. Management has a clear plan and a realistic timetable.

## Earnings impact

We expect the deal to add to earnings per share from 2027, once the first synergies come through and the bulk of integration spending is behind the bank. The larger client base should also support pricing power and improve operating leverage over time.

## Recommendation

We reiterate our Buy rating and our price target of CHF 64. In our view, the merger turns Talvero into one of the most attractive mid-sized private banking franchises in Switzerland, and we see the current share price as a good entry point for long-term investors.
