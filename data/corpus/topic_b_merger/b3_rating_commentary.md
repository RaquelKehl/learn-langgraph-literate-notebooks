# Helix places Talvero Bank on watch negative after merger announcement

Helix Credit Ratings (fictional) has placed the A rating of Talvero Bank AG (fictional) on watch negative following the announcement of its planned merger with Nordquist Privatbank AG (fictional).

## Transaction terms

Talvero Bank AG, headquartered in Zurich, and Nordquist Privatbank AG, headquartered in Lucerne, announced an all-share merger on 20 October 2025. Nordquist shareholders receive 0.85 Talvero shares for each Nordquist share. The implied transaction value of CHF 1.2 billion is based on this exchange ratio. The combined bank will have combined client assets of CHF 48 billion. The banks target annual cost synergies of CHF 65 million by the end of 2028 and expect one-off integration costs of CHF 90 million. Completion is expected in the first half of 2026, subject to regulatory and shareholder approval. The combined bank will be called Talvero Nordquist AG (fictional) and will be led by Talvero's CEO Daniel Aebischer.

## Rating considerations

Helix considers the CHF 65 million target to be ambitious. The banks will need to merge two separate core banking systems, a process that is complex, costly and prone to delay. Integrations of Swiss banks have historically been accompanied by cost overruns, and Helix sees a material risk that integration spending will exceed initial plans. In addition, private banking relationships depend heavily on individual advisers, and there is a risk of losing private banking clients during the transition.

Helix does not question the strategic logic of the combination. However, the agency is not yet convinced that the synergy target is reachable within the stated timeframe, and execution risk weighs on the credit profile in the near term.

## Next steps

Helix expects to resolve the watch once there is greater clarity on the integration plan and on the outcome of the approval process.
