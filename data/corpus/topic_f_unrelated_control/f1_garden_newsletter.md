# Autumn newsletter 2025

Dear friends of the Seewil Community Garden Association (fictional),

What a season it has been in our little village garden. As the beds are cleared and the first frosts arrive, here is a look back at the highlights of the year.

## Bake sale

Our summer bake sale was the best yet. Thanks to your cakes, biscuits and jams, the bake sale brought in revenue of CHF 1,240 in 2025. The money will go towards new tools and a second water butt for the herb beds.

## The pumpkin patch

The pumpkin patch enjoyed record growth in the third quarter of the gardening year. Warm days in July and August and plenty of compost gave us more pumpkins than ever before, and several of them were too heavy to lift without help.

## Harvest competition

The results of the harvest competition are in. First prize for the largest pumpkin went to the Huber family, the prize for the finest tomatoes went to our youngest member, Mia, and the jury awarded a special mention to the shared bean bed by the gate.

## Looking ahead

Winter digging days will take place on the first Saturday of each month. Bring gloves and a flask of tea, and join us in preparing the garden for spring.

Warm wishes from the committee.
