# New club regulation on safety equipment

The committee of the Seewil Cycling Club (fictional) has adopted a new regulation on safety equipment for all club rides.

## New requirements

The new requirements for members are as follows:

- A helmet must be worn on every club ride, whatever the distance or the weather.
- Every rider must use front and rear lights after dusk.

## When the regulation applies

The regulation applies from 1 January 2026. Until then, the committee asks all members to check their equipment and replace worn or missing items in good time.

## Why we are doing this

Several near misses on our evening rides last winter showed that visibility and protection matter for everyone in the group. Ride leaders will remind members of the new requirements at the start of each ride and may ask riders without the required equipment to sit out the ride.

Questions about the regulation can be raised with the committee at the next club evening.
