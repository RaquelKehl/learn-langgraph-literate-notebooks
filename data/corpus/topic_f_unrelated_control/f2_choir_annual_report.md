# Annual report of the Seewil Mixed Choir 2025

The year 2025 will be remembered as a turning point in the history of the Seewil Mixed Choir (fictional).

## Annual general meeting

At this year's annual general meeting, the members agreed the merger of the Seewil Mixed Choir with the Oberried Men's Choir (fictional). The proposal had been discussed at several rehearsals over the spring, and the vote in favour was clear. The singers of both choirs agreed that joining forces would give everyone a richer repertoire and a fuller sound.

## The combined choir

The combined choir now has a membership of 54 singers. Rehearsals take place every Thursday evening in the village hall, alternating between the two rehearsal rooms we used before. Our conductor has already started to prepare pieces for mixed voices that neither choir could have sung on its own.

## Looking ahead

The first joint concert is planned for spring 2026. The programme will bring together folk songs from both villages, sacred music and a few lighter pieces for the audience to sing along with.

The committee thanks all singers, helpers and friends of the choir for their support in this special year, and warmly invites new voices to come and listen at a rehearsal.
